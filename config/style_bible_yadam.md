# Channel style bible — Joseon yadam manhwa

## Line work
- 2-3 px bold tapered ink contours, thinner 1 px interior lines for folds and hair
- Clean confident strokes, no sketchy hatching, no photographic texture

## Character design
- Realistic proportions (adults 7-7.5 head heights, children by age), expressive faces with clear eyebrows and mouths
- Late-Joseon hanbok drawn with flowing folds; topknots, braids, headbands and hairpins always visible

## Coloring
- Flat color fills with two-tone cel shadows (one shadow step), soft gradient only in the sky
- No 3D render look, no semi-realistic painting, no film grain

## Background treatment
- Simplified painterly backgrounds behind characters; clear tonal separation between figure and background
- Period-accurate thatched/tiled houses, earthen kilns, narrow snowy ravines; blank wood and blank paper only

## Quality markers
- Faces and clothes match the reference sheets; each character appears exactly once
- Story action is readable at phone size; no text, letters or logos anywhere
- 16:9 full-bleed single illustration, no panel borders
